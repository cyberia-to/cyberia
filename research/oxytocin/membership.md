# membership

the relationship layer that converts visits into a compounding trust account. one membership, one space, every stage.

---

## what membership replaces

a member's Ubud week typically distributes across: a café for work, a restaurant for dinner, a spa for recovery, a coworking space, a laundry service, a pharmacy, a kids program. each relationship is thin, transactional, replaceable. [[oxytocin]] collapses this scatter into one operator who knows the member's habits, preferences, and needs. switching costs accumulate as the relationship deepens.

---

## tier structure

thermal — access to the [[thermal-circuit]] (sauna, hammam, cold plunge), drinks counter. the entry tier. active from [[stage-1]].

nourishment — thermal access plus the coffee bar and deli. daily visit rhythm begins. active from [[stage-2]].

full — thermal, F&B (restaurant, bar), [[universal-chair-zone]] bookings, market. the complete lifestyle tier. active from [[stage-3]].

resident — full access plus coworking floor, kids zone, and [[services-layer]] (laundry, concierge, telemedicine, mailbox). the membership that organizes a week. active from [[stage-4]].

---

## pricing logic

membership pricing sits above the sum of casual per-visit rates and below the sum of scattered alternatives. the value is the integration: one place, one bill, one relationship. per-visit rates remain available — membership converts guests who return more than twice a week.

---

## compounding

each stage adds surface area to the membership value. a thermal member in [[stage-1]] becomes a nourishment member when the coffee bar opens — the upgrade requires no new decision, only a billing change. the relationship already exists.
