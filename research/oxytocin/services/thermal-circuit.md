# thermal circuit

the three-phase thermal protocol that anchors the first stage of [[roadmap]] and underlies every subsequent stage of [[oxytocin]].

---

## the three elements

sauna — dry radiant heat, 80–95 °C. opens pores, dilates vessels, elevates core temperature. wood-paneled interior, helo heater. existing infrastructure.

hammam — wet steam, 40–50 °C. blue mosaic tile. sustained heat at lower intensity, gentler cardiovascular demand. existing infrastructure.

cold plunge — 10–15 °C immersion, 1–3 minutes. vasoconstriction, norepinephrine spike, the reset. the new addition that completes the circuit.

---

## the protocol

one cycle: heat phase (sauna or hammam, 12–15 min) → cold plunge (1–3 min) → rest (10–15 min). three cycles constitute a full session.

the sequence is the product. the individual elements are components — the circuit delivers the full physiological response: elevated oxytocin release, improved heart rate variability, reduced cortisol.

---

## capacity and scheduling

a single circuit session accommodates 4–6 guests. two sessions per day in stage-1. the constraint is the cold plunge — one installation services one session simultaneously.

the drinks counter (coffee, cold press, [[adaptogen teas]]) runs continuously between and after sessions — the thermal anchor draws the revenue tail on every visit.

---

## relationship to membership

the thermal circuit is the entry point of the [[membership]]. every tier includes thermal access. the circuit is the trust-building loop: guests who come for heat and cold return as members who stay for everything else.
