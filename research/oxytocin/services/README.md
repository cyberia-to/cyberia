# services

the eight life-logistics services that activate in stage-4 of the [[roadmap]] and convert the resident [[membership]] from a lifestyle choice into a dependency.

---

## the eight services

laundry — drop-off and pickup on the member's schedule. priced below Ubud laundry street rates, above [[oxytocin]]'s cost. members hand over a bag and retrieve clean clothes within 24 hours.

logistics — package receipt at a real address, courier coordination, storage for arriving deliveries. removes the friction of receiving shipments as a nomad.

concierge — bookings, transport coordination, recommendations. the human layer that handles requests the app layer cannot.

crypto — on/off-ramp (cash to crypto and back), hardware wallet consultation, peer support. priced per transaction. makes [[oxytocin]] legible to the crypto-native resident population in Ubud.

telemedicine — scheduled video appointments with vetted practitioners: GP, dermatologist, physiotherapist. removes the cost and distance of a Denpasar clinic visit for non-emergency care.

mailbox — a postal address for members who rotate between Bali and elsewhere. receives mail, holds packages, scans and forwards on request.

pet — grooming appointments, vet coordination, boarding referrals. removes the coordination overhead of pet ownership in Ubud.

repair café — electronics repair, clothing alteration, object restoration. fix-first before replace. one specialist attends one day per week on a sessioned model.

---

## the compounding logic

each service the member uses creates a recurring visit decoupled from discretionary mood. a member whose laundry runs on Tuesday and Friday returns Tuesday and Friday regardless of whether they feel like coffee or a thermal session. the services layer generates daily touch-points that F&B and the [[thermal-circuit]] cannot manufacture by themselves.

---

## revenue model

laundry and logistics generate daily transaction volume — the highest-frequency revenue line in the building. telemedicine and crypto generate occasional high-value revenue. the repair café runs on a specialist session model: the specialist's fee is covered by bookings, the margin is [[oxytocin]]'s.

the resident [[membership]] tier bundles a monthly allocation of services into the flat fee. high-usage members pay per-service overage. the bundle converts volume users into predictable recurring revenue.
