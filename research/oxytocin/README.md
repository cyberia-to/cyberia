# oxytocin

500 sqm in Sayan, Ubud. one threshold where the scattered necessities of daily life — café, restaurant, spa, coworking, childcare, laundry, logistics — converge under one operator and one [[membership]].

the name is the molecule: the hormone of trust, bonding, and calm presence. the space is engineered to produce it.

---

## the four stages

see [[roadmap]] for the full build sequence.

stage-1 — The Body. the [[thermal-circuit]] anchor. revenue from week one.

stage-2 — Nourishment. the full coffee bar and deli. the destination becomes a daily habit.

stage-3 — The Table and the Edit. restaurant, market, [[universal-chair-zone]], retail. the lifestyle environment complete.

stage-4 — The Operating System. coworking, kids zone, [[services-layer]]. the place a week is organized around.

each stage is a complete, bookable experience before the next is built. capital is protected by sequential cash-flow proof.

---

## the model

one [[membership]] spans all stages. the membership competes with the sum of every subscription, booking, and errand a member would otherwise scatter across the district. switching costs accumulate as one place handles more of a life.

revenue runs across five streams at maturity: [[thermal-circuit]] sessions, F&B (the daily traffic layer), the [[universal-chair-zone]] (highest margin per sqm), the market, and the [[services-layer]] (the recurring revenue that compounds).

see [[economics]] for the full financial model.

## location

Sayan, Ubud — the ridge above the Ayung River gorge. the physical address where the first [[stage-1]] infrastructure already exists: a sauna and a hammam.
