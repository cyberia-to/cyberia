# research

how a question becomes a page of knowledge with evidence. the question is a task of kind question; the answer is a page in `research/` or `docs/` of the owning repository, with every claim marked by its evidence word — open, simulated, measured, proven.

trigger: a question raised by any process — a unit returned three times, a claim nobody can mark, a proposal that needs a foundation, the seer's own curiosity.

## the graph

| # | node | kind | role | makes | green when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | frame | splitter | seer | the question split into sub-questions that can each be answered independently | every sub-question names what evidence would settle it |
| 2 | search | workers | one body per sub-question, each in its own context, reading pages and code before writing | an answer with sources | every claim carries a source line: a page, a file and line, a measurement |
| 3 | converge | worker + critic | a chair merges, a disagreement list follows, rounds go one body at a time | one page | a round that changes nothing, or the third round |
| 4 | keep | gate | keeper | the page placed where its readers are, linked, aliased | the page resolves every wiki-link, and the ledger names where its parts went |

## return edges

- short: a claim without a source goes back to search for that sub-question only.
- long: a settled answer that changes a contract leaves a line in the specification; a refuted projection is rewritten where it stood, never left beside its refutation.

## lanes

all contained: a page of knowledge is reversible by its history. the one wide case is a page that changes the charter's wording, which goes to [[govern]].

## pages

the task of kind question; the page of knowledge; the convergence log on the task. what remains disputed after three rounds goes to the owner as a verdict, written on the task.
