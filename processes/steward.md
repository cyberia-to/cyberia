# steward

how sigma — what the state holds in every form of capital — is counted, and how budgets open or close lanes. from [[org]]: sigma = financial + physical + digital + relational; the entity survives while sigma stays above its threshold.

trigger: a period ends; a lane asks for budget; a holding changes hands; a vendor quota or a bill arrives.

## the graph

### 1 · count · code node

- role: counter
- makes: sigma by form, from sources that can be re-read: ledgers, chains, registries, invoices
- green when: every line has a source and a date

### 2 · budget · worker

- role: seer proposes, counter checks
- makes: a budget per process and per body for the period: tokens, minutes, money
- green when: the sum fits sigma's flow; every lane has a cap

### 3 · open · gate

- role: counter, the owner above the threshold
- makes: lanes opened or closed for the period
- green when: a sensor on each cap fires before the cap, not after

### 4 · report · code node

- role: counter
- makes: the portfolio page and the state board's sigma
- green when: the page re-derives from the sources

## return edges

- short: a line without a source returns to count.
- long: a cap that was crossed becomes a tighter sensor; a lane that never used its budget gives it back.

## lanes

counting is contained. opening a budget is wide. spending above the threshold the owner set, moving a holding, or signing on the state's behalf is irreversible and passes the owner.

## pages

`portfolio/` of the entity: sigma by form; the budgets as goals with sensors; the reports in `audit/`.
