# market

how a released product reaches people: who hears of it, understands it, and joins. the measure is cap — the one vital sign that cannot be gamed from inside — and the count of neurons that arrive and stay.

trigger: a release from [[deliver]]; a beat of the chronicle; a moment on the calendar — a launch, a genesis, a gathering.

## the graph

### 1 · story · worker + critic

- role: seer writes, keeper grades against the charter
- makes: the one sentence and the one page for this release
- green when: every claim in it is a claim on the product page, marked

### 2 · make · workers

- role: runner
- makes: the artifacts: the deck, the post, the thread, the clip, the mentor link
- green when: each artifact passes its own harness: fits every screen, every number marked, every link resolves

### 3 · send · gate

- role: the voice of the state
- makes: the artifacts out through the doors: cyberia.to, the blog, x.com, the channels
- green when: the owner's hand on the public voice; the chronicle records what went out

### 4 · count · code node

- role: counter
- makes: arrivals, passages, windows from lytics; mentions; the market
- green when: numbers with their source and method

## return edges

- short: a number that does not match its claim returns the artifact to make.
- long: what people understood and what they did not goes to [[listen]] as signals, and to the seer as a constraint on the next story.

## lanes

the public voice is wide: anything said in the state's name passes a human. a deck on a preview domain is contained; the same deck replacing a product's home is irreversible in the public's memory and passes the owner.

## pages

the story and the artifacts in the product's repository under `landing/` or the chronicle; the counts in `audit/`; the beat in the blog.
