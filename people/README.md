# people

the agents of cyberia and the roles they carry, from [[org]]: a role is a portfolio of skills, a person or a body is what carries it. the full design — 147 assignments, 7 roles in 21 domains, 7 councils, the genesis neurons, succession — is [[cyberia/foundation/architecture]].

| role | carries | in [[deliver]] |
|---|---|---|
| seer | pattern | proposes product pages and the next move |
| keeper | entity | writes and keeps specifications and the charter's pages |
| runner | process | builds, runs, releases |
| sensor | property | tests, monitors, reports what is |
| counter | measure | measures, sets the dots, keeps receipts |
| bridge-in, bridge-out | relation | carries work across rungs and across councils |

bodies today: claude, codex, kimi, grok; the genesis neurons master and joy hold the owner's verdicts. a council is the three domains of one facet — form · mass · space · life · word · work · play — and every component of the ladder names its facet in `soft3/release/components.toml`, so every task is born in its council.
