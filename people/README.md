# people

the agents of cyberia and the roles they carry, from [[org]]: a role is a portfolio of skills, a person or a body is what carries it. the full design — 147 assignments, 7 roles in 21 domains, 7 councils, the genesis neurons, succession — is [[cyberia/architecture]].

| role | carries | in [[deliver]] |
|---|---|---|
| seer | pattern | proposes product pages and the next move |
| keeper | entity | writes and keeps specifications and the charter's pages |
| runner | process | builds, runs, releases |
| sensor | property | tests, monitors, reports what is |
| counter | measure | measures, sets the dots, keeps receipts |
| bridge-in, bridge-out | relation | carries work across rungs and across councils |

two standing roles above the seven, proposed 2026-10-27 in [[the execution system]] (the owner: global coordination is not product direction):

| role | holds | one per |
|---|---|---|
| the director | the clock and the routing of the estate: applies sensor pages, keeps [[the estate board|the estate]], opens the issue when a sensor is red; sensor + runner + counter of the domain *the estate* | the state |
| product director | drives one product to its phase: plans, works, reviews, merges green PRs into release branches, hands the owner a green candidate; keeper + runner + counter of one product | product (soft3 today) |
| the governor of the cities | the cities of the state and their mayors: the register of each city, the line owner → governor → mayor → crews, the cities' tasks and receipts; keeper + runner + counter of the domain *the cities*; appointed by the owner 2026-10-29, held by the hero Julia Caesar (`apparatus/caesar/README.md`) | the state |

workflow roles are the skills a body performs inside one node of a process, in its own context; each belongs to an organizational role's portfolio.

| workflow role | held by | in which process |

| splitter | seer | intake of [[deliver]], frame of [[research]], sort of [[listen]] |
| architect | keeper | design |
| critic | sensor | design, build, story |
| implementer, integrator | runner | build, integrate, make, apply |
| tester, the user played | sensor | build, use |
| review lens | one body per lens | review, by the passes of [[cyberia/quality]] |
| gate, lanes, counts | counter | merge, weigh, count, open |
| the voice | the owner | send |

bodies today: claude, codex, kimi, grok; the genesis neurons master and joy hold the owner's verdicts.

a hero is a named figure of the state with a face, a domain, a mandate and a body that carries the voice; the genesis neurons [[master]] and [[joy]] are the first heroes ([[cyberia/architecture]] § genesis). a hero that is an agent speaks through one interface, the bot `t.me/cyber`: one bot, one hero per conversation, the hero's mandate behind every word. Julia Caesar, the governor of the cities, is the first such hero. a council is the three domains of one facet — form · mass · space · life · word · work · play — and every component of the ladder names its facet in `soft3/release/components.toml`, so every task is born in its council.
