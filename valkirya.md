# valkirya

the school of empresses of [[cyberia]]: a ten-year formation path for women who want to lead — to govern land, tokens, people and their own life. from kitchen to cosmos, from career to lineage.

## nine resources

every student learns to acquire, allocate, deploy and account for nine resources.

| triad | resources |
|---|---|
| inner — what she is | body, energy, soul |
| interface — how she engages | mind, connections, time |
| outer — what she works with | information, tokens, territory |

energy runs from personal stamina through territorial power to the [[Kardashev scale]]. soul generates purpose, purpose generates energy — the circuit that keeps everything else alive.

## territory

every domain is walked with her own hands: a woman who has run a process sees what it costs and where it optimizes.

| tier | domains |
|---|---|
| A — daily cycle | cleaning, cooking, kids, animals, supply |
| B — craft | harvesting, soft pruning, banya, massage |
| C — infrastructure | hard pruning, wood, repair, delivery, concrete, stone, groundwork |
| D — leadership | management, total oversight |

four levels in each domain: execute → sustain → coordinate → teach.

## four quests

business → land → garden → house. first flow, then ground, then seasons, then permanence.

## weekly rhythm

report · friction log · request · assignment. the friction log is the core: it shows honesty and learning.
