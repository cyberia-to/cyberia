# valkirya NDA

a simple non-disclosure agreement. it is the step that opens the closed sections of [[valkirya]].

version 2026-10-12

## 1. parties

- discloser: Joy, founder of [[valkirya]] and [[cyberia]], telegram [@st_joy](https://t.me/st_joy)
- recipient: the woman who signs this agreement

## 2. confidential information

the closed sections of the valkirya school paper — the oath, the four dimensions, the three completions, the six metrics and the growth architecture — and everything the discloser shares about them, in any form: text, voice, video, conversation.

## 3. obligations of the recipient

1. she keeps the confidential information to herself
2. she uses it for one purpose: to decide whether she joins the school
3. she keeps it away from third parties, publications, recordings and AI services
4. on request she deletes or returns every material she received

## 4. what stays free

the obligations cover information that is confidential at the moment of disclosure. they leave free:

- what is public, or becomes public through no act of the recipient
- what the recipient lawfully held before disclosure
- what a court or state body lawfully demands — the recipient tells the discloser first, where the law allows

## 5. term

the agreement holds for twenty years from signing. the obligations of section 3 hold for the same twenty years, also after the recipient leaves the school or declines it.

## 6. breach

a breach closes the recipient's path in the school. the discloser keeps the right to claim damages under the governing law.

## 7. law and reach

the agreement is multinational. it acts on the whole planet Earth and in major blockchains, Ethereum and Bitcoin first among them.

a claim goes, at the claimant's choice, to the courts of any country where the recipient lives, works or holds assets, under that country's law, or to international arbitration. a signature made with a key is evidence in each of these venues.

## 8. signing

the recipient signs in one of two ways and sends the result to [@st_joy](https://t.me/st_joy) in a private message:

- with her key — a signature over the text of this version
- by hand — on the PDF of this agreement, as a scan

the discloser countersigns and returns it. each side keeps one copy. signed copies live privately and never enter the graph.
