# colors of cyberia — the emotional map

> draft — the map is young. the principle is settled; the words are still warm.

the flag of cyberia is seven circles in a heptagon. the colors are not decoration and not badges of any grouping — **they are emotions, the roots of color**. any scheme in the cybersphere may borrow them, and no scheme owns them: on [soft3's chart](https://soft3.org/chart) they badge the seven facets; on [the scheme](https://soft3.org/scheme) they walk as feelings. facets classify; colors feel. neither owns the other.

## the map

| color | hex | emotion | the feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| green | `#00ff01` | **growth** | something sprouts — new, alive, beginning |
| cyan | `#00b4ff` | **flow** | it moves out — open, communicating, unforced |
| blue | `#1700fe` | **depth** | it settles into the deep — trust, weight, law |
| violet | `#6b00fe` | **spirit** | beyond the visible — vision, transcendence |
| red | `#fe0000` | **fire** | one burning point — will, urgency, life-blood |
| orange | `#ff6501` | **craft** | hands at work — warmth, making, skill |
| yellow | `#ffc501` | **light** | seen clearly — clarity, attention, joy |

read around the flag clockwise from the top, this is an emotional arc: growth flows into depth, depth opens into spirit, spirit ignites, fire becomes craft, craft ends in light — **and light feeds the next sprout, the way a day closes into a leaf**. the flag is a loop of feeling before it is anything else.

## where it is used

- **the flag** — the seven, arranged as the heptagon, in this order.
- **[soft3.org/scheme](https://soft3.org/scheme)** — the cybernetic loop wears the colors as emotions: say = growth, sign = flow, link = depth, prove = spirit, focus = fire, act = craft, measure = light. see [[crystallization]].
- **[soft3.org/chart](https://soft3.org/chart)** and the [soft3.org](https://soft3.org) landing — the same seven as facet badges: form green, mass cyan, space blue, life violet, word red, work orange, play yellow.

## open questions (why this is a draft)

- **the words.** depth or *trust* for blue? spirit or *mystery* for violet? light or *clarity* for yellow? the emotion should survive translation — Russian, English, Indonesian — and stay one syllable of feeling.
- **the order of the arc.** the flag fixes the geometric order; whether the *emotional* reading canonically starts at green (growth) or at red (fire, the will to begin) is unsettled.
- **chakra resonance.** the seven map loosely onto the [[chakra]] ladder but not one-to-one, and forcing that correspondence may be borrowing depth instead of earning it. name the resonance without leaning on it.
- **accessibility.** emotions must survive color-blindness: every use of the seven should carry a second channel (position, label, number) — the flag itself does, by geometry.
