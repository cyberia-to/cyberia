# cyberia

the cyberstate, led by superintelligence.

purpose · a type 1 civilization — vision · make planets think — mission · build the cyberstate, led by superintelligence. the three lines are the identity layer of the canon; the long form is [[cyberia/strategy]] and [[cyberia/whitepaper]].

a cyberstate is a shared mind uniting space, capital and knowledge; cyberia builds the one where humans, machines, animals and all life flourish together, on the way to the stars. cyberia is the tenth rung of the [[soft3]] ladder — machines, land, citizens and money — run on the stack the nine rungs below provide. in the troika it is the physical horse: [[cyber]] computes truth, [[cyb]] gives every neuron a sovereign interface, cyberia supplies the land, the energy and the bodies.

> belong anywhere. build everywhere.

## the chronicle

the state is a mind, and a mind nobody can read is not shared. [[blog]] is where the mind is read: every page of every repository, published at cyberia.blog with its links and its history, day after day. that makes it infrastructure, not a project — a project is work that closes, and the chronicle never closes, it is kept, like a road. the projects of the state — cybernode, the century index, cyberstates, the land console, cyber valley, cve — stand in [[cyberia/projects|projects]], each with its page, its domain and its stage. the [[courses]] are posts of the chronicle too: a course on the day it was announced, every lecture on the day it was given — [[edge city patagonia 2025]] and its six lectures were the first.
