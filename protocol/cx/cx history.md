## drift

quantities never change, so shares do. the basket was written at 35% crypto, 30% fiat, 35% elements; a decade of bitcoin and ether outrunning everything else leaves it at 98.0% crypto today. this is the arithmetic of fixed quantities, not a flaw in the fixes — but over a 25-80 year lease it is the property that decides what the obligation actually tracks. the T4 review valve exists for exactly this question, and it moves at most one leg of at most 10% weight every fifth year.
