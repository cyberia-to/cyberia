# apparatus

the repository of the estate: every machine of the state with its configuration, the servers' runbooks, the agents and their mandates, the cockpit, the heroes of the state and their voices. it is private; its pages are mounted under rockets for the people who run the machines. the node of the network is a different thing: [[cybernode]], rung 7 of the ladder; the repository that used to carry the machines under that name was renamed apparatus on 2026-10-27 when the node left soft3 (soft3 `proposals/cybernode.md`).
