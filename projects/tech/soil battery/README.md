# soil battery

electrochemical power from the ground — copper, salt, and [[andosol]] soil driving a diode so a trail can glow at night without grid or solar panels on the path.

## idea

bury a simple cell in the path bed. the wet volcanic soil is the electrolyte. a copper electrode and a salt bridge / salt-doped zone make a weak but continuous voltage. an LED (diode) on that voltage marks the trail — soft ground-level light, not a lamp post.

use case at [[cyber-valley|cyber valley]] / [[sinwood]] / night trails: edge markers, steps, and fork cues that run as long as the soil stays moist.

## stack

| part | role |
|------|------|
| [[andosol]] | electrolyte medium — high organic matter, sponge water retention, ion exchange; Gesing soil stays conductive when damp |
| copper | anode / electrode — oxidizes slowly, releases electrons into the circuit |
| salt | ionic booster — NaCl or local salts raise conductivity of the soil pore water |
| diode (LED) | load — converts the micro-current into visible glow on the path |

optional: second electrode (zinc, graphite, stainless) as cathode; series cells for brighter LEDs; diffuser stone or glass over the diode so the light is a point on the ground, not glare.

## electrochemistry (short)

1. moisture in andosol dissolves salt → mobile ions  
2. copper (and paired electrode) create a potential difference in that ionic medium  
3. circuit closes through the diode → photon emission  
4. as long as soil moisture + salt + metal contact remain, the cell keeps a trickle current  

power is tiny (milliamp / low-volt class). design for markers, not floodlight. many cells along a trail beat one big battery box.

## why andosol

Gesing [[andosol]] holds water like a sponge and has high cation exchange — better natural electrolyte than dry sand or compacted clay. that is why soil-powered glow fits this land specifically: the dirt is part of the device.

## fit in the stack

- night trail amenity without stringing AC along the path  
- pairs with [[magic forest]] / bioluminescence: soil LEDs = engineered markers; fungi = living glow  
- cousin of [[battery]] / [[water battery]] — same electrochemistry idea, substrate is soil not a sealed pack  

## open experiments

- copper vs zinc–copper couple voltage in wet Gesing andosol  
- salt dose vs plant safety (keep salt local to the cell pocket, not broadcast on beds)  
- diode type (red / amber low-Vf) for reliable light at ~0.5–1.5 V stacks  
- lifetime: electrode corrosion rate in tropical wet season
