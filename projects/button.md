# button

the owner, 2026-11-18: sales of leasehold begin with a button on the global map of [[cyberia.my]]. a buyer finds a plot on the globe, presses the button and pays, by bank or by crypto. behind the button the back office runs the legal documents of every move: shares, HGB, leasehold and hak milik rights. it runs in full compliance, and every process it depends on works. the critical part is that any owner can add their own plot, and so can an agent, which is harder but important; Gesing, [[cyber valley]], is the pilot. the document pack and the due diligence are automated as far as they can be. priority: top 3.

## what the button sells

each plot carries one right, and the button sells the instrument that fits it. the table is the working map; each row is confirmed with the notary (PPAT) before it is sold with the button.

| the plot's right | who may hold it | what the button sells | where it is registered |
|---|---|---|---|
| hak milik (freehold) | Indonesian citizens only | a leasehold (hak sewa) from the owner, upfront or annual | the lease deed with the notary; the certificate stays with the owner |
| HGB (right to build) held by the company | a PT, including a PT PMA | a leasehold from the company, or the plot's share in the company | the lease deed; a share move through the GMS and AHU |
| HGB to be granted on hak milik | a PT on an Indonesian owner's land | a leasehold now, HGB after the grant | the land office (BPN) after the grant |
| hak pakai (right of use) | foreigners resident in Indonesia, and others | the right of use where the law allows it | the land office (BPN) |

a move of shares (between holders, or into the company as a plot's contribution) goes through the company's own instruments: the GMS resolution, the share sale deed, the waiver of pre-emption, and the report to AHU. it is never a side agreement.

## the parts

1. the button on the globe: a plot opens its card with its right, its area, its price, its terms and its documents, and one button that leads to payment and signing.
2. payment: by bank, as a transfer to the company's account in rupiah or an international wire; by crypto, in stablecoins to the company's address. every payment is matched to its plot and its buyer, receipted, and accounted for in the company's books and taxes.
3. compliance: the buyer's identity (KYC), sanctions and AML screening, the source of funds, the limits on foreign ownership, and the taxes of each instrument (on a lease, on a transfer of HGB, on a share sale). each one is a check that runs before the buyer can sign, never after.
4. the back office: every instrument, every share move and every right is a record with its document, its status (drafted, signed, notarised, registered) and its next step, so nothing is lost between the buyer, the notary, the land office and the company.
5. add your plot (the critical part): the owner uploads the certificate, the Surat Ukur (the survey map) and their identity, then draws or confirms the polygon on the globe. the system checks the certificate against the land office and the zoning (PKKPR), and the plot is listed once the checks pass. Gesing goes first.
6. agents: an agent lists a plot for its owner under a mandate the owner signs, with the commission written into the mandate. this is harder (two parties, and the mandate must be proved), but it is how the land of others reaches the map.
7. the document pack and the due diligence: the land office's non-encumbrance search ordered through the PPAT, the zoning printouts from OSS, the tax receipts (PBB), the certified copies, and the pack assembled from them. the cve due diligence memo is the script to automate, step by step.
