# launch century index

the project that takes the [[century index]] from a protocol page to a unit people owe in: the index published every day under its own name, and the first lease priced in CX signed. the index itself — the basket, the fixes, the collar — is the protocol page; this page is only the order of delivery.

## the delivery

1. the index publishes 365 days a year at [cx.cyberia.my](https://cx.cyberia.my) from public fixes, with its history and the annex — done 2026-11-16 (the daily fix had failed since 2026-09-29 on a dead gold source; gold now comes from PAXG daily closes, LBMA as the fallback).
2. the first lease priced in CX signed — [[development|cyber valley]] instrument B is the first consumer; the annex of that lease is the model annex of the protocol page § 6.
3. the on-chain daily fix (phase 2 of the protocol page: Goldilocks fixed-point, provable) — after the launch of the chain.
