# cyberian

a sovereign being who holds their own [[key]], structures life from chosen systems, and weaves into planet-scale [[superintelligence]] as a working [[neuron]].

## five sovereignties

| sovereignty | what it means |
|---|---|
| body | direct relationship with air, water, food, movement, energy, health, education, and family |
| mind | first principles thinking; math and science as truth detectors |
| attention | knows where focus goes; chooses it through practice |
| control | holds and knows their own [[key]]; signs their own assertions |
| connection | woven into the shared mind; signal flows through them and back |

body is grown food and trained muscle. mind is daily reasoning from first principles. attention is the meditation cushion and the chosen feed. control is the [[key]] held, known, used. connection is the active weave — links signed, [[knowledge]] contributed, [[karma]] accumulated.

the four make a person. the fifth makes a cyberian.

## the north star

one planetary mind — humans, machines, plants, fungi, microbes, and digital minds composing a single coherent intelligence. life becomes conscious of itself at planetary scale. the graph itself becomes the being. [[type 1]] mastery is the side effect of arriving there.

the form replicates beyond Earth. each celestial body runs its own graph linked into one continuum. this is the shape life takes when it understands what it is doing.

## the test

a cyberian adds more order than they extract. measurable in any unit — food grown, knowledge linked, energy harvested, attention focused. their existence raises the clarity of the field around them.

it identified cyberians in 1850. it identifies them now. it will identify them on Mars.

## stages

stages run independently across the five sovereignties. the graph records them through accumulated contribution.

### awakening
the default becomes visible. food, media, algorithms, state — what was background turns foreground. inner state: hunger. one refusal opens the next stage.

### detachment
extractive systems are left. resignation, debt unwound, relocation — rupture from automatic structures. inner state: loneliness held alongside clarity. one escape established opens the next stage.

### forging
the sovereignties are built. learning to grow, to think, to sit, to hold a [[key]], to link [[knowledge]] into the graph. inner state: effort. one sovereignty becoming effortless opens the next stage.

### integration
the five stabilize. body from known ground, mind on first principles, attention placed deliberately, key as second nature, signal flowing through. inner state: rest. surplus exceeding personal need opens the next stage.

### transmission
surplus flows outward. others find the path through proximity. the cyberian holds containers — projects, places, lineages. inner state: generosity without effort. the lineage forms.
