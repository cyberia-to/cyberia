# calendar

the time of the organisation, kept in one place: the clocks it lives by, the rhythm each clock carries, and the events ahead. the cockpit draws it at [apparatus.cyberia.my/calendar](https://apparatus.cyberia.my/calendar); this page is its source. a [[calendar]] aligns days to astronomical cycles; this one aligns the work of [[cyberia]] to them.

## the clocks

five clocks run at once, each at its own speed. a neuron of the organisation knows where it stands in each.

| clock | cycle | what it carries |
|---|---|---|
| block | seconds | the chain's own time: each block is an irreversible tick of [[cyber]] |
| sun | the day, 24 h, counted in UTC | the candidate of the release train at 12:00; the day's work |
| week | seven days | one standing review a day, the table below |
| moon | 29.53 days, new moon to new moon | money is counted by the moon: the books close at the new moon, the report goes out at the full moon ([[lunar-based]], [[new and full moon]], `cve/management.md` § moonly processes) |
| season | the year: dry from April, wet from September on the land of [[cyber valley]] | the harvests, the season's preparation, the year's assessment |

## the rhythm

what returns, and when. a row is a standing duty of its clock; the cockpit lights the one whose time is now.

| clock | point | what | who |
|---|---|---|---|
| week | Monday | asset planning: the week's work plan | keepers, runners |
| week | Tuesday | lease review: payments due, arrears, leases expiring | counters |
| week | Wednesday | maintenance cycle: the preventive schedule | tech-runner |
| week | Thursday | knowledge review: pages to create, update, link | keepers |
| week | Friday | council sync: priorities across domains, conflicts | councils |
| week | Saturday | financial review: the rent roll, the cash position | counters |
| week | Sunday | metabolic review: which asset needs attention | seers |
| moon | new moon | the books close: rent roll, payroll, the moon's costs | counters |
| moon | first quarter | the pipeline: inquiries, term sheets, signings | keeper, seers |
| moon | full moon | the moon report goes out; the shares are verified | bridge-out |
| moon | last quarter | the inventory: species, equipment, replacements | counters |

## the events

what is ahead and happens once, or once a year. a row lands here by a signed command in the cockpit (`put on the calendar 2027-02-14 "the event"`) or by a commit. `when` is a day (`2027-02-14`), a day and an hour in UTC (`2027-02-14T12:00`), or a month of every year (`every April`). what the cockpit already knows — a project's date, a domain's expiry, the treasury's last day, a model's window — is drawn from its own source and is never copied here.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2027-01-01 | test event | event | — | — |
